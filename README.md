# TinyTotBooks 📚

So... I'm a biology student.

My sister has a small children's book business, and at some point I thought:

**“I'll just make her a website.”**

That sentence was the beginning of a very unnecessary rabbit hole.

What started as *“let me put some books online”* slowly turned into a proper little bookstore with a storefront, book pages, cart, bundles, inventory, checkout, Razorpay payments, orders, tracking... basically everything I kept thinking *“eh, might as well add this too”* to.

I didn't really know what I was doing when I started.

I mostly **vibecoded my way through it** — building something, breaking something, staring at an error for an embarrassing amount of time, fixing it, and then somehow creating three new problems in the process.

There were quite a few **2 AM debugging sessions**, questionable life choices, and moments where I genuinely wondered why I didn't just make a static page with a phone number on it.

But somewhere along the way, it became an actual working thing.

And honestly, that's what makes this project fun.

It's not some huge startup.
It's not a college assignment.
It's not even remotely related to biology.

It's just a real thing that someone actually needed, and I decided to build it.

Which is also kind of funny because **med school is right around the corner**, so I have absolutely no idea why I decided that *now* was the appropriate time to start a random ecommerce side quest.

Maybe I'll look back at this in a few years and wonder what the hell I was doing.

For now, I'm just glad I built it.

### The stack

`HTML` · `CSS` · `JavaScript` · `Supabase` · `PostgreSQL` · `Razorpay` · `GitHub Pages`

Still a work in progress.

Still adding random ideas.

Still breaking stuff.

Still fixing stuff at 2 AM.

And probably still going to open this repo occasionally during med school and think:

**“Wait... I actually made this?”**
